## 📋 Steps to Play

1. **Open** http://localhost:3000 in browser
2. **Click** "Login / Play Now"
3. **Enter** your name and roll number
4. **Read** the MCQ question (20 seconds)
5. **Click** "Next / Start Game"
6. **Control snake** with arrow keys or touch D-pad
7. **Eat the correct answer** option
8. **Repeat** for 10 questions
9. **View** final score and leaderboard
